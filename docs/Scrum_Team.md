# Scrum Team

| Student | Scrum Role |
|---|---|
| Student 1 | Product Owner |
| Student 2 | Scrum Master |
| Student 3 | ML Engineer |
| Student 4 | Data Engineer |
| Student 5 | Tester |

If one student performs the experiment, that student assumes all five roles.

## Responsibilities
- **Product Owner:** Defines requirements, prioritizes work, maintains the Product Backlog, and accepts completed work.
- **Scrum Master:** Conducts Sprint Planning and Daily Scrum, removes blockers, and helps the team follow Scrum practices.
- **ML Engineer:** Selects the algorithm, trains and improves the model, and saves the trained model.
- **Data Engineer:** Loads and prepares data, checks data quality, and prepares features.
- **Tester:** Tests the model, checks evaluation results, reports bugs, and validates outputs.

**Example product requirement:** Develop an Iris Flower Classification model with at least 95% accuracy.
